New York to Nairobi with a stopover

New York (JFK) to Nairobi (NBO) is about 11,837 km (7,355 miles) — roughly 16h 45m of flying nonstop. Splitting the journey with a few days in a stopover city breaks up the jet lag, lets you see a second destination, and booking each leg separately can open up low-cost airlines.

How to book New York to Nairobi with a stopover

  1. Pick your stopover. Casablanca, Lisbon, Algiers are the most direct choices on this route.
  2. Choose your dates. Decide how many nights to spend in the stopover city — 2 to 4 nights is typical.
  3. Book each leg. Plan My Trip links every flight to Google Flights and the airlines likely to fly it. Separate tickets are not protected if a delay makes you miss the next flight, so leave a day or more between legs.

Some airlines also sell multi-city tickets or free stopover programmes through their hub, which keep both flights on one booking — worth comparing with separate tickets.
