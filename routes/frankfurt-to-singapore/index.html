Frankfurt to Singapore with a stopover

Frankfurt (FRA) to Singapore (SIN) is about 10,279 km (6,387 miles) — roughly 14h 40m of flying nonstop. Splitting the journey with a few days in a stopover city breaks up the jet lag, lets you see a second destination, and booking each leg separately can open up low-cost airlines.

How to book Frankfurt to Singapore with a stopover

  1. Pick your stopover. New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru are the most direct choices on this route.
  2. Choose your dates. Decide how many nights to spend in the stopover city — 2 to 4 nights is typical.
  3. Book each leg. City Juggler links every flight to Google Flights and the airlines likely to fly it. Separate tickets are not protected if a delay makes you miss the next flight, so leave a day or more between legs.

Some airlines also sell multi-city tickets or free stopover programmes through their hub, which keep both flights on one booking — worth comparing with separate tickets.
