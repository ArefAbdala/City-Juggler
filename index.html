    Plan a multi-stop trip in minutes

    City Juggler is a free planner for trips with several cities. Enter where you start and finish, add up to four city stops — or let it suggest stopover cities along your route — and get at least eight itinerary options sorted by estimated cost. Each leg links to Google Flights and to the airlines that fly it, so you can book every flight separately.

    How are stopover cities suggested?

    Stops are picked from 110 major hub airports, ranked by how small a detour they add between the neighbouring points of your route. You can prefer the same or a different country, choose a continent, and list cities, airports or countries to avoid.

    Are the prices real?

    No — they are rough distance-based estimates to help you compare routes. Real prices and availability are shown on Google Flights and airline websites. Always confirm before booking.

    Do I need an account? Is my data stored?

    No account is needed. Everything runs in your browser; your last search is saved only in your own browser, and nothing is sent to a server.
